1) Warm — Dubai, United Arab Emirates
- Why it fits: Hot desert climate with abundant sun; great for sun-soaked days, modern city experiences, and desert adventures.
- Best time to visit: October to March (pleasant daytime temps ~24–30°C; evenings cooler).
- Top things to do: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, desert safari, beaches, upscale dining, and shopping.
- Quick tips: Dress modestly in some areas, stay hydrated, and plan indoor/outdoor activities to beat peak heat in summer.

2) Cozy — Goa, India
- Why it fits: A relaxed, laid-back vibe with warm-to-mild days and breezy coastal charm.
- Best time to visit: November to February (pleasant 20–30°C); during monsoon (June–September) it’s rainier and lusher.
- Top things to do: Beach time at Calangute/Palolem, Portuguese-influenced architecture in Panjim, spice plantations, seafood feasts, sunset on the river cruises.
- Quick tips: Try local seafood curries, explore spice farms, and consider a quieter off-season area for a cozier feel.

3) Humid — Malaysia
- Why it fits: Humidity is high year-round with tropical rainforests, lush landscapes, and diverse cultures.
- Best places to start: Kuala Lumpur (city food scene), Penang (historic George Town and amazing street food), Borneo (Kinabatangan, Danum Valley, Sepilok for orangutans).
- Best times to visit: Generally drier pockets vary by coast; the west coast (Penang, Langkawi) is drier Nov–Feb; overall outlook is humid year-round.
- Top experiences: Rainforests, wildlife sanctuaries, fantastic cuisine, beaches, and island hopping.
- Quick tips: Stay hydrated, carry a light rain jacket, and plan indoor-tempo activities during peak humidity.
